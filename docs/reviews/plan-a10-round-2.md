## Disposition

- **BLOCKING 1.** `install-cyrus.sh` generates `GITHUB_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `GITLAB_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `SLACK_SIGNING_SECRET` on the box. They go into the root-only env file and are never printed. The webhook fail-closed probes, including `/mcp/cyrus-tools`, are added to acceptance (A10 §6, acceptance).
- **BLOCKING 2.**
  - Sandbox readability checks are added: `test -r`, `git credential fill` (password masked) and the `--show-origin` origin.
  - The `/var/lib/gh-token` tmpfs `.mount` fallback is named.
  - `/run/gh-token` is created by `tmpfiles.d`.
- **Non-blocking items.**
  - The `gh` wrapper moves to the service-only `/usr/local/libexec/cyrus-bin`.
  - The mirror uses `After=` with a freshness check instead of `Requires=`.
  - The helper takes a `write|read` argument, and `includeIf` uses absolute paths with a no-symlink note.
  - The minter mints, checks scope, then writes.
  - Acceptance adds the `environ` check and `git log` author.
  - The `CYRUS_API_KEY` mention is dropped from A9(b).

Re-submitted as round 3.
